# Predicted satellite passages over the Karoo: the May 2024 storm, 10 to 12 May

Retrospective for the May 2024 storm, 10 to 12 May (the Gannon storm's main phase and first recovery, inside the benchmark's disturbed interval), at the MeerKAT array phase centre (-30.7111, 21.4439, 1087 m; 13.5 m dish). Positions are public element sets propagated with SGP4, selected by latest archived epoch before the cutoff, no older than 7 days. Publication timestamps were not reconstructed. No beam classification accuracy has been calibrated by the component table. Nothing here is a received power, an occupancy fraction or a sensitivity loss.

**Population and limits.** 507,302 element sets for 25,658 objects with epochs from 2024-04-30 to 2024-05-12, from Space-Track's gp_history. Constellation membership is by catalogue name (GLONASS by owner and orbit) and includes retired members, because the catalogue does not carry transmit status. Emissions are declarations from public filings, dated in `docs/radio-emissions.md`; a declaration made after these observations (Starlink direct-to-cell, November 2024) is still listed as later declared capability, not demonstrated operation in April or May. Component diagnostics require an eligible reference mission, explicit manoeuvre-excluded scope and an age from 6 to 168 h; altitude alone does not establish eligibility. 400-600 km (460 to 507 km): GRACE-FO 1 (C), GRACE-FO 2 (D), Swarm A, Swarm B, Swarm C; sets 95 quiet, 91 May 2024, 100 October 2024, 93 August 2024; 600-750 km (696 to 719 km): CryoSat-2, Sentinel-1A; sets 45 quiet, 41 May 2024, 42 October 2024, 39 August 2024; 750-850 km (783 to 803 km): SARAL, Sentinel-3A, Sentinel-3B; sets 57 quiet, 65 May 2024, 51 October 2024, 60 August 2024; 850-1000 km (893 to 952 km): HY-2C, HY-2D, SWOT; sets 78 quiet, 77 May 2024, 60 October 2024, 65 August 2024; 1000-1400 km (1338 to 1338 km): Jason-3, Sentinel-6A; sets 37 quiet, 30 May 2024, 32 October 2024, 35 August 2024 No crossing or beam-timing guarantee is attached to these catalogue passages.

## Observations

Existing public-record illustration. No new archived schedule or satellite detections have been added.

No archived observation with a public record was found for this period. The SARAO archive carries the pointing, start, duration and band of every released observation and documents a GraphQL API for reading them, which needs a logged-in account's token (`driftwatch radio archive`, `docs/radio-lane.md`); no such token was available to this run, and no published circular or paper read for this work gives a MeerKAT observation inside these days. Product two therefore has nothing to run on here; product one is computed below for the whole period, hour by hour, because it needs no pointing. `driftwatch radio period` runs both products the moment an observation list is exported from the archive.

This geometric visibility count has no sidelobe gain or power estimate and needs no schedule.

## What this does not show

- These are modelled passages through the stated pointing; there is no measured satellite detection here.
- Schema 2 removes crossing_horizon and position_horizon: orbital component errors do not determine beam-entry or timing classification. A full-vector topocentric comparison is required.
- orbit_quality requires an eligible reference mission, explicit manoeuvre-excluded public-GP scope and measured age. Altitude overlap alone never supplies a calibrated uncertainty.
- Epoch age is not publication age. Catalogue availability at observation start is not reconstructed.
- radio_band is declared emission-frequency evidence, not the beam frequency, actual transmission, received power or interference. Per-object capability and historical operation may be unknown.
- candidate_discontinuity is an element-change heuristic. The former fit_arc_spanned_manoeuvre field is withdrawn: the assumed exclusion arc is not a published fit arc.
- Where the archive phase centre is unavailable, a target position stands in for it. Scan boundaries, calibrator scans and slews are unknown, so the assumed pointing is not an actual schedule.
- Historical circular-aperture passages are retained as illustrations. The measured-beam comparison uses constructed fixed celestial pointings, explicitly separate from this public-record example.
