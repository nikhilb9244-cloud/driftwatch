# Monthly reference comparison

The job runs on the twelfth day of each month at 04:43 UTC and can be dispatched manually. It checks completed UTC calendar months in reverse order, beginning with the immediately preceding month. The search stops at the newest month with reconstructed orbit coverage for at least one of the fifteen v2 reference missions. The twelve-month search bound fails explicitly if no month qualifies. A failed provider request is unknown availability; it cannot justify silently moving to an older month when no mission has confirmed coverage.

Before each candidate month's availability check, the job exclusively creates a dated protocol naming all candidate missions, the month and required reference interval, the endpoint, fit cutoff, lead grid, exclusions and population-selection rule. Orbit availability uses reconstructed states and product coverage, with no SGP4 residual computation. A separately hashed population record then names the eligible missions and accounts for every excluded or unknown mission. Actions retains these records before the scoring step. Both hashes are checked before acquisition and scoring. Any scoring failure or mission without trials prevents a completed claim; no failed mission is removed after residual inspection.

The scientific checkout is fixed at commit `fedc1bc7f398edf6bbff4b8c066f95c8f2865cd9`, with its dependency lock and unchanged v2 truth-RIC covariance method. The wrapper refuses a changed checkout or an import from another source tree. The comparison uses public GP history, reconstructed orbits, the v2 manoeuvre exclusions and descriptive deletion checks. It remains an epoch-based reconstruction. This job does not add a storm, radio, learned-model or density experiment, and it does not adopt a recipe.

Each completed month produces a separate evidence object containing the v2 reference fields: definition, population, missions, windows, per-band and per-mission results, full lead denominators, component coverage and deletion sensitivity. Its tables and claims record are rendered from that object. [The version index](assets/claims-versions.json) appends its hashes beside the existing v2 record. The original evidence, claims and public v2 wording remain unchanged. Existing months cannot be overwritten or pooled into v2. The monthly contract test checks every indexed evidence hash, claims record and rendered table.

Protocols, evidence, tables and the version index are committed under `docs/assets/`. Raw provider inputs remain outside Git; the evidence retains source identifiers and content hashes. Derived per-trial Parquet files are retained in the run's Actions artifact, with their SHA256 and run identifier in the evidence. Actions artifact retention is ninety days; the durable Git record retains the complete statistical evidence and tables after that retention period. A new public archive of per-trial data would require an explicit retention decision.

Tests exercise month boundaries, leap years, protocol-before-probe ordering, changed protocol and population rejection before residual access, unknown availability, missing population accounting, the v2 empirical endpoint, append-only versioning and rendered-text checks. The first scheduled observational run is separate from these synthetic contract tests.
